# Productside Playbook: choose a Play

A Play is a guided exercise for a piece of product work. Start with the question or decision in front of you, then choose how you want help. You do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.

## Two ways to use a Play

| I want to... | Start here | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Get coaching, critique, or rehearsal for a task | playbook-prompts.md | Find the Play below, copy its prompt into a chat, and replace the bracketed context. Nothing to install. |
| Work through the full method and produce its artifact | playbook-skills.md | Find the same Play's instructions, embedded template, examples, and quality checks. In a Project, ask the assistant to use that reference. |

A prompt is a bounded conversation. A skill is the fuller guide to the Play. You can use either route, switch between them, and bring equivalent prior work without completing earlier Plays.

## Choose how to work

| Mode | What it means |
|---|---|
| Guided | Complete the work and learn the method one step at a time. Get coaching and context-relevant best-guess recommendations when training was missed, forgotten, or interrupted. |
| Expert | Bring your context, draft, or artifacts and ask for the result. Ask for guidance on any step when needed, then continue without restarting intake. |
| Discovery | Let the assistant synthesize missing context and quickly sketch the outcomes you seek. Get just enough guidance for a simulated walkthrough, with assumptions visible and listed for validation. |

You can switch modes during a conversation. Supplied context is reused. Every mode closes with a summary of what was supplied, synthesized, and still unresolved, then hands the decision back to you. Guided recommendations are options to consider, not answers assumed on your behalf. Discovery does not require a complete brief; simulated outcomes remain distinct from interviews, team agreement, or actual results. The Blueprint navigator helps first rather than forcing an intake form.

## Try it

> "Help me choose a Play. Our warehouse supervisors stop using our scheduling tool after their first week. Guide me one question at a time."

> "Use Play 05 to critique my draft job statements. Find its coaching prompt first; use the skill reference if we need the template or format rules."

## Using the three files

In a ChatGPT Project, add this guide, playbook-prompts.md, and playbook-skills.md as sources and use the supplied Project instructions. They are reference documents; no agent skills are installed by uploading them. Start a new chat for each distinct outcome.

Outside a Project, copy a prompt into your AI chat, or attach the individual Play's SKILL.md from the repository. In an agent tool that supports installed skills, use the corresponding skills/ps-.../ folder and that tool's installation instructions. Each folder contains only SKILL.md.

## Evidence, ownership, and missing references

AI-generated customer details are hypotheses, not research. People conduct real interviews, experiments, and team sessions, and own decisions. Record only evidence you have and distinguish it from assumptions. If the assistant cannot locate a needed source or section, it should name what is missing and ask for it, not invent Productside instructions. Keep unrelated clients or products in separate Projects.
