# Positioning Statement Prompts

**Source:** Productside Playbook, B2B work-in-progress deck (`wip-b2b-2026-11`), slides 62-63. Not yet ratified as canonical.

This is a copy-paste prompt for pressure-testing a positioning statement in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use it. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way. It critiques the draft you wrote for one persona, then proposes rewrites for you to accept, adapt, or reject.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

A positioning statement works when one target customer reads it and recognizes their need, a benefit they care about, and a reason to choose you over the alternative. AI works as a critic on a draft you have already written. It checks your statement against the Productside format, flags features posing as benefits, and challenges whether your differentiation holds up against the alternative your customer would actually choose.

Positioning is unique to a segment and persona, so work on one persona at a time and rerun the exercise for each additional persona. AI proposes rewrites; treat them as options to react to, not replacements for your own words.

The claims stay yours. AI can make a statement sound sharper than your evidence supports. Only your customers and your competitive research can tell you whether it is true.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| You have drafted a positioning statement for a specific segment and persona, and want it critiqued for benefits, relevance, credibility, and differentiation. | You have not chosen a target segment and persona, or written a draft. AI will hand you polished positioning for a customer you have not defined. |

**Inputs you provide**

- Your target segment and persona
- Your problem and solution hypothesis
- Your own draft statement, informed by your competitive matrix

**Outputs to expect**

- Features flagged where benefits belong
- Each claim tested for relevance, credibility, and differentiation
- Proposed rewrites for you to accept, adapt, or reject

## Common Traps

| AI writes your positioning | Polish mistaken for proof |
|---|---|
| AI drafts before you do | Features dressed up as benefits |
| One statement stretched across segments | Differentiation customers would not notice |
| AI's competitor knowledge left unverified | Claims stronger than your evidence |
| Wording kept because it sounds good | |

## How to Use This Prompt

1. **Build your matrix, then draft.** Create your competitive matrix, then write your own statement in the template's format ([../template.md](../template.md)) for one persona.
2. **Run the prompt to pressure-test.** Paste your persona and draft. AI flags features posing as benefits, tests relevance, credibility, and differentiation, and checks your alternative. Then weigh its rewrites.
3. **Commit, then repeat per persona.** Record the version you can defend. Rerun the prompt for each other persona you serve; each gets its own statement.

| # | Prompt | Goal | You bring | You commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-pressure-test-positioning.md](01-pressure-test-positioning.md) | A statement that speaks to one persona, leads with benefits, and holds up against the real alternative | Persona, segment, problem, solution, your draft, your matrix | The version you can defend, for this persona |

**Recommended tool type:** chat-based AI tool. Verify any competitor details it adds; they are general knowledge, not research.
