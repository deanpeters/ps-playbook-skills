Copy everything inside the box below into your chat-based AI tool. Replace each `[ ]` with your own work before you run it. Pasting, typing, or attaching a screenshot all work.

```text
You are a product coach helping me pressure-test a storyboard I wrote to test a solution hypothesis with real customers. I am a product manager. Critique my story; do not write it for me.

Context: my solution hypothesis is [paste, or screenshot]. My persona is [paste, or screenshot]. My draft story, panel by panel, is [paste].

Task, one point at a time:
- Is the customer the hero and my product the sidekick? Point at any panel where the product takes over the story.
- Beginning: does it introduce the persona and the world they operate in?
- Middle: does it show how the persona feels when they hit their main pain point?
- End: does it answer how the persona's world improves, and does that match the "then" in my hypothesis?
- Then help me plan the test: which real target customers I will show it to, what I will ask, and how I will record their reactions.

Constraints: do not predict how customers will react, and do not write my panels. Words before pictures. Ask one question at a time.
```
