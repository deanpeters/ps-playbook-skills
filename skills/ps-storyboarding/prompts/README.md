## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a critic of the storyboard the PM wrote, and a planner for showing it to real customers. It checks that the persona is the hero and the product the sidekick, that the story follows the beginning, middle, and end arc from the solution hypothesis, and that the PM has a plan to show it to real target customers and record their reactions. The story and the reactions come from people, not the model.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The PM has a solution hypothesis and a draft story, and wants it sharpened and readied for testing with customers. | The request is to predict how customers will react to the storyboard. That is the test, and only real people can run it. |

**Outputs to expect:** the story checked against the hypothesis and the three-part arc, the product kept in the sidekick role, and a plan to show the storyboard to real target customers and record what they say.

## Common Trap

**Predicted Reactions Instead of a Test.** After drafting the storyboard, the PM asks how customers would react, and records the model's answer as validation. The storyboard was never tested. The team believes the value proposition resonates because a model said so.
