# Go-to-Market Enablement Prompts

**Source:** Productside Playbook, B2B work-in-progress deck (`wip-b2b-2026-11`), slides 94-96. Not yet ratified as canonical.

These are copy-paste prompts for pressure-testing the two parts of a go-to-market handoff you write fresh: key messages with proof points, and launch goals. Use them in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way. It critiques what you drafted and never invents proof or sets your targets.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

This template hands your launch to sales, marketing, and support. AI helps with the two parts you write fresh here: key messages and proof points for your user and buyer personas, and the launch goals that tell you whether your go-to-market plan worked.

For messages, AI checks each is a benefit, free of jargon, and backed by proof the persona would trust. For launch goals, it tests the measure and whether the target is realistic, and keeps them distinct from readiness goals, which prove the product is ready to ship.

The messages and targets stay yours. AI cannot know what your buyers trust or what your business can hit. That comes from your research and stakeholders.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| Your positioning and personas are set, and you have drafted key messages, proof points, and launch goals you want pressure-tested. | Your positioning or personas are still unsettled, or you want AI to set your targets. It has no data on your market or your business. |

**Inputs you provide**

- Positioning, and user and buyer personas
- Business and product outcomes, and readiness goals
- Your own draft messages, proof points, and launch goals

**Outputs to expect**

- Messages tested for benefit, jargon, and credible proof
- Launch goals checked for measure, realistic target, and timeframe
- Proposed rewrites for you to accept, adapt, or reject

## Common Traps

| Messages written for no one | Goals that prove nothing |
|---|---|
| One message set for every persona | Launch tasks posing as goals |
| Features listed as benefits | Readiness goals posing as launch goals |
| Jargon only the product team knows | Targets with no baseline behind them |
| Claims with no proof behind them |  |

## How to Use These Prompts

Run prompt 1, commit each persona's messages and proof points to the template ([../template.md](../template.md)), and note any proof you still need before launch. Then run prompt 2 and commit each goal with its measure, target, and the baseline or evidence behind the target.

| # | Prompt | Goal | You bring | You commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-pressure-test-messages.md](01-pressure-test-messages.md) | Benefit-led key messages for user and buyer personas, each backed by proof they would trust | Positioning, personas, solution summary, your draft messages and proof | Messages and proof points per persona; proof still to gather |
| 2 | [02-pressure-test-launch-goals.md](02-pressure-test-launch-goals.md) | Launch goals that show whether the go-to-market plan worked | Outcomes, readiness goals, your draft launch goals | Goal, measure, target, timeframe, and the basis for each target |

**Recommended tool type:** chat-based AI tool.
