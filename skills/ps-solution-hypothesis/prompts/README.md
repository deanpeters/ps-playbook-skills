# Solution Hypothesis Statement Prompts

**Source:** authored for this library. Not yet in the Productside Playbook. This Play is Template 12 of 24 in the B2B work-in-progress deck (slides 57-59), which has no AI page for it. The prompt follows the style of the deck's own AI prompts and apply its global rule.

This is a copy-paste prompt for working this Play in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use it. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a critic of the solution hypothesis the PM drafted. It checks the If/Then format, tests whether the customer-focused hypothesis chains into the business-focused one, flags features posing as benefits, and asks whether each risk and assumption is specific enough to test. The solution and the bet stay the PM's.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The PM has a validated problem statement and a prioritized solution, and has drafted the hypothesis. | There is no validated problem statement. A hypothesis about an unframed problem has nothing to test against. |

**Outputs to expect:** the If/Then chain tested link by link, benefits separated from features, risks and assumptions sharpened into things a test could settle, and proposed rewrites for the PM to accept, adapt, or reject.

## Common Trap

**AI Writes the Bet.** The PM asks for a solution hypothesis and gets a complete, confident If/Then chain with benefits, risks, and a business outcome. No one on the team chose the bet. When the test results come in, nobody knows which link was the real assumption.
