The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a facilitation coach before the session and recorder after it. It helps the PM prepare quadrant prompts and protect psychological safety, then turns the team's own items into specific, owned action items. The items come from the team; the agent never writes what the team experienced.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The PM is preparing to run a retrospective, or has the team's raw items to organize into action items. | The request is to write the team's Wind, Anchors, Beacons, or Barriers without a session. |

**Outputs to expect:** discussion prompts for each quadrant, moves that keep the room safe, and action items with an owner and a date, built only from the team's items.

## Common Trap

**A Retrospective Without the Team.** The PM asks the agent to "fill in the retro for last sprint" and gets plausible items in all four quadrants. The team's real anchors stay unspoken, and the action items fix problems nobody named. The retro's main value, team trust, never happens.
