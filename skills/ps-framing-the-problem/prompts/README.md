# Framing the Problem Prompts

**Source:** authored for this library. Not yet in the Productside Playbook. This Play is Template 10 of 24 in the B2B work-in-progress deck (slides 46-48), which has no AI page for it. The prompt follows the style of the deck's own AI prompts and apply its global rule.

This is a copy-paste prompt for working this Play in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use it. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a critic of the problem frame the PM drafted. It checks each stem of the I am / Trying to / But / Because / Which makes me feel sequence for customer voice and evidence, catches a solution hiding in the frame, and tests whether the final statement synthesizes the stems. The frame stays the PM's, built from their discovery evidence.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The PM has discovery evidence and a draft frame, and wants it pressure-tested before the decision point. | There is no discovery evidence yet. A well-formed frame on a guessed problem is still a guess. Route to ps-problem-identification or ps-empathy-interviews. |

**Outputs to expect:** each stem checked for customer voice, specificity, and evidence; solution language flagged; and the final statement tested against the stems.

## Common Trap

**A Frame With No Evidence Under It.** The PM asks for a problem frame for a persona they have not interviewed, and gets five well-formed stems and a crisp statement. The frame passes the decision point on its polish. The team commits to a problem no customer confirmed.
