Copy everything inside the box below into your chat-based AI tool. Replace each `[ ]` with your own work before you run it. Pasting, typing, or attaching a screenshot all work.

```text
You are a product coach helping me pressure-test a problem frame I drafted. I am a product manager. Critique my draft; do not write a new one.

Context: my persona is [paste, or screenshot the worked template]. My problem identification and interview findings are [paste, or screenshot]. My draft frame is:
I am [ ]. Trying to [ ]. But [ ]. Because [ ]. Which makes me feel [ ].
My final problem statement is [paste].

Task, one stem at a time:
- Is it in the customer's voice and about their world, not our product team's?
- Is it specific enough to act on? Point at any generic words.
- What evidence from my interviews backs it? If I have none, mark it as a hypothesis.
- Is a solution hiding in it, especially in "Because"? Point at the word.
- Does "Which makes me feel" name an emotion I actually heard, or one I assumed?
Then check that my final statement synthesizes the stems and does not introduce anything new.

Constraints: do not invent evidence, quotes, or emotions. Offer rewrites only after the critique, as options for me to accept, adapt, or reject. Ask one question at a time.
```
