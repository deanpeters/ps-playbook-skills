The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a reviewer of the canvas before the Go/No Go decision. It checks that each section is consistent with the upstream work it summarizes, that the user and buyer stories are grounded, that outcomes and metrics are measurable, and that the Ask is specific. Every claim on the canvas must come from the PM's work, because leadership will decide on it.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The PM has assembled the canvas from upstream artifacts and wants it reviewed before presenting it. | The upstream work doesn't exist yet. A canvas assembled from guesses becomes a Go decision on a guess. |

**Outputs to expect:** inconsistencies between sections flagged, stories checked against evidence, outcomes and metrics checked for measurability, and the Ask sharpened into a specific decision and resource request.

## Common Trap

**A Canvas Built From Nothing.** The PM asks for a Product Outcome Canvas for a new idea and gets every section filled: stories, metrics, features, risks, and an Ask. Leadership makes a Go/No Go decision on a document that summarizes work nobody did.
