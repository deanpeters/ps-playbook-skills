# Risk Assessment Prompts

**Source:** authored for this library. Not yet in the Productside Playbook. This Play is Template 15 of 24 in the B2B work-in-progress deck (slides 72-74), which has no AI page for it. The prompt follows the style of the deck's own AI prompts and apply its global rule.

This is a copy-paste prompt for working this Play in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use it. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a a widening and pressure-testing partner for the risks the PM's team brainstormed. It asks whether the list covers enough ground, offers risk categories (and, after the PM answers, candidate risks marked as candidates), and checks that each mitigation, contingency, and owner is real. Severity, likelihood, and the plan stay with the team.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The team has brainstormed risks and wants the list widened and the management plan pressure-tested. | The request is for a finished risk register for a product the team hasn't discussed. The risks that matter are the ones only the team can see. |

**Outputs to expect:** gaps in breadth named, candidate risks offered as candidates, each mitigation separated from its contingency, and each risk given a real owner.

## Common Trap

**A Risk List the Team Never Discussed.** The PM asks the agent for six risks and gets a tidy, plausible list with severity, likelihood, and mitigations filled in. The list covers generic risks and misses the ones only the team knows. Nobody owns the mitigations, because nobody chose them.
