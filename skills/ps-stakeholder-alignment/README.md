## Skill type

Component. This skill produces a working artifact: a stakeholder table, an Interest/Influence matrix, and a risks and gaps section.

## Where it sits in the playbook

Second play, Context phase. It runs before empathy interviews and discovery work begin.

The stakeholder map tells the PM who to involve, who to brief, and who to watch. It is a prerequisite for empathy interview recruiting (you need to know who to talk to) and for solution space exploration (you need to know who to include).

## Key distinction

The stakeholder map covers people **inside the organization** who affect whether the initiative succeeds.

Customers who use the product in the field belong in the persona play (ps-proto-persona), not here. Mixing the two groups creates confusion about who needs organizational engagement vs. who needs product discovery.
