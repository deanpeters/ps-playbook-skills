# Jobs-to-Be-Done & Outcomes Prompts

**Source:** Productside Playbook, B2B work-in-progress deck (`wip-b2b-2026-11`), slides 21-23. Not yet ratified as canonical.

These are copy-paste prompts for working through the Jobs-to-Be-Done & Outcomes Play in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use them. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way. Bring it your draft and it will pressure-test the draft instead of starting over.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI is a sharp second reader for your jobs and outcomes. After you draft a first pass, it can check each entry against the format, flag a job that is really an outcome or a solution, and push you to add the emotional and social jobs you skipped.

What AI cannot do is tell you what your customers are trying to accomplish. Jobs come from real people in a real context, not from a model. Any job or persona AI produces is a hypothesis to validate through Voice-of-the-Customer, never a finding to present as fact.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| You have a target segment and a drafted list of jobs and outcomes to pressure-test for format and contamination. | Your target segment is still vague or unnamed. Without a real persona and context, AI will invent generic jobs that validate nothing. |

**Inputs you provide**

- Your target segment from Market Selection & Sizing, or any equivalent segment definition
- First-pass job statements in functional, emotional, and social format
- Any research or Voice-of-the-Customer notes you already have

**Outputs to expect**

- Each job passed as clean, or marked as contaminated with an outcome or solution
- Missing emotional and social jobs named, not written for you
- Outcomes checked for direction, metric, and context

## Common Traps

| AI fabricates job statements | Blurred jobs and outcomes |
|---|---|
| Plausible jobs with no customer behind them | An outcome mislabeled as a job |
| A generic list that fits anyone | A solution dressed up as a job |
| A persona that is a stereotype, not research | "Optimize," "enhance," or "improve" passed off as a direction |
| Hypotheses handed back as findings | |

## How to Use These Prompts

Run them in order. Commit your work to the template ([../template.md](../template.md)) before moving to the next prompt.

1. **Set the context.** Review and capture your work from the Market Selection & Sizing template, or whatever defines your target segment.
2. **Draft first.** Write your functional, emotional, and social job statements yourself, then your product outcomes.
3. **Run the prompt.** Paste it into your chat tool and fill every `[ ]` with your own work. Pasting, typing, or attaching a screenshot all work.
4. **Commit what survives.** Keep the edits you agree with, reject the ones you don't, and record the result in the template.

| # | Prompt | Goal | You bring | You commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-check-job-statements.md](01-check-job-statements.md) | Build a clean set of job statements | Segment + drafted job statements | Job statements in the three Productside formats |
| 2 | [02-check-product-outcomes.md](02-check-product-outcomes.md) | Build a clean set of product outcomes | Segment + drafted outcome statements | Outcomes with direction, metric, and context |

**Recommended tool type:** chat-based AI tool.
