# Empathy Interviews Prompts

**Source:** Productside Playbook, B2B work-in-progress deck (`wip-b2b-2026-11`), slides 33-34. Not yet ratified as canonical.

These are copy-paste prompts for preparing for empathy interviews in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use them. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way. It suggests topic areas instead of scripting your questions, reviews the questions you draft, and runs a labeled rehearsal on request.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI helps you walk in prepared. It focuses you on what's important so you can guide the conversation, and it can play a sparring partner so you can rehearse your technique before it counts.

What AI cannot do is be the person you are interviewing. The whole point of this Play is the real conversation. The moment you ask AI to answer as your customer, you have a script, not research, and the Play is corrupted.

Rehearse with AI, then go have the real conversation and listen. The words that matter come from a person, not a model.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| You have real interviews scheduled, and you want to sharpen your questions or rehearse your technique before you sit down with someone. | You are tempted to have AI answer as the customer. A simulated interview is not research; it's practice. |

**Inputs you provide**

- The segment and persona you are exploring (Market Selection & Sizing, Jobs-to-Be-Done & Outcomes, Personas & Proto-Personas)
- Your learning objectives, and the decision the interviews will inform
- Your draft questions or topic areas, written to pull stories, not yes/no answers

**Outputs to expect**

- Leading or closed questions identified for you to rewrite
- Each kept question tied to a learning objective
- Your follow-up reflexes drilled against realistic interview moments

## Common Traps

| AI plays the customer | AI biases the interview |
|---|---|
| Role-play treated as research (synthetic customers to interview) | AI writes leading questions |
| Generated answers logged as findings | Questions fish for your solution |
| The real conversation gets skipped | No learning objective behind the questions |
| | You read the question list robotically |

## How to Use These Prompts

Run them in order. Answer honestly, including "I don't know yet," and commit something before moving to the next.

| # | Prompt | Goal | You bring | You commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-prepare-interview-guide.md](01-prepare-interview-guide.md) | Walk in with sharp, unbiased questions | Your persona, learning objectives, and the decision the interviews inform | 4-5 topic areas and your own open-ended questions, recorded in the template ([../template.md](../template.md)) |
| 2 | [02-rehearse-technique.md](02-rehearse-technique.md) | Practice the technique to follow the story wherever it goes | Your persona | Nothing to your findings. Take away where you slipped and what to do differently. |

Then go talk to a real customer.

**Recommended tool type:** chat-based AI tool. Voice mode is recommended for prompt 2.
