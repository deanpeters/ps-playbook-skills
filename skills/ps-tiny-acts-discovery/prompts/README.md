# Tiny Acts of Discovery Prompts

**Source:** Productside Playbook, B2B work-in-progress deck (`wip-b2b-2026-11`), slides 83-85. Not yet ratified as canonical.

These are copy-paste prompts for building a living backlog of assumptions and designing small, fast acts to test them, in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use them. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way. You score and rank the backlog and pick the act; it sharpens and challenges.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

Tiny acts of discovery is the habit of keeping a prioritized backlog of assumptions about your product and chipping away at them with small, fast acts of learning, over and over. A tiny act can be almost anything: one survey, a single question to a single user, a willingness-to-pay test, a quick prototype. The craft is finding the shortest path to a signal.

AI works as a thought partner across the loop. You collect the opportunities and name the assumptions beneath them; AI widens your view and tests how confident you really are. You pick what to learn next.

The certainty score is yours, because only you know how much evidence you hold. If AI rates your confidence for you, your backlog is built on its guesses, and you cannot tell anyone why this, why now.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| You have a pile of opportunities, ideas, or signals and want a prioritized backlog of assumptions plus fast, cheap ways to start testing them. | You are using the backlog to look busy. If no act ever gets run, AI is helping you plan instead of discover. Planning isn't the point; discovery is. |

**Inputs you provide**

- Opportunities and signals from problems, solutions, tickets, and sales calls
- Your own first cut at the assumption beneath each one
- How much real evidence you hold for each

**Outputs to expect**

- Raw opportunities turned into testable assumptions
- Your certainty and impact ranking pressure-tested
- A range of fast, creative ways to start learning

## Common Traps

| AI scores the backlog for you | The backlog replaces the discovery |
|---|---|
| AI rates your certainty | A tidy list, no conversations |
| Confidence claimed without your evidence | Planning becomes the activity |
| The 2x2 becomes AI's guesses | Acts defined, never run |
| You can't explain why this ranks here | Looks strategic, learns nothing |

## How to Use These Prompts

Re-run prompt 1 whenever new evidence arrives, and keep the backlog where stakeholders can see it. Record each act in the template ([../template.md](../template.md)), then go run it.

| # | Prompt | Goal | You bring | You commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-rank-assumption-backlog.md](01-rank-assumption-backlog.md) | Turn a pile of opportunities into testable assumptions ranked by impact and certainty | Opportunities, your first-cut assumptions, your evidence | The ranked backlog, scored by you |
| 2 | [02-design-tiny-act.md](02-design-tiny-act.md) | Find the fastest, cheapest act that produces a real signal | One high-impact, low-certainty assumption and the decision it informs | The act, its measure, and its owner |

**Recommended tool type:** chat-based AI tool. Prompt 2 asks it to search the web for more kinds of tiny acts.

## Source Notes

- The deck rates **certainty** (how much evidence you hold). The baseline template's 2x2 rates **uncertainty**. They are the same axis read in opposite directions: high impact and low certainty is the same as high impact and high uncertainty.
