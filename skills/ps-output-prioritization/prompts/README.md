# Output Prioritization Prompts

**Source:** Productside Playbook, B2B work-in-progress deck (`wip-b2b-2026-11`), slides 89-90. Not yet ratified as canonical.

This is a copy-paste prompt for preparing to facilitate an output prioritization session, in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use it. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way. It prepares you to lead the room, then records the placements the room makes. It does not rank your features.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

Prioritization is a conversation about trade-offs, not a calculation. AI can help you prepare for that conversation. It rehearses the session with you: how to set up the room, how to anchor every feature against a single center-point output so the ranking stays relative, and how to keep the group honest when every idea starts drifting into high-value, low-cost territory.

The plotting belongs to the room. Real cost comes from your tech team, real value from customer evidence, and the call on what to build first comes from you and your stakeholders together, not the tool.

What you get from AI is a sharper facilitator, not a ranked list. You walk in ready to lead the tradeoffs, not to read out an answer.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| You have a list of outputs and need to run a session where stakeholders prioritize them by relative value and cost. | You want AI to score and rank the features. It has no real cost data and cannot create team buy-in. |

**Inputs you provide**

- Your list of potential outputs from your discovery efforts and release plan
- The product outcomes these outputs should drive (Outcome Tree or Outcome-Based Roadmap)
- Who will be in the room, including tech to scope cost

**Outputs to expect**

- A session plan anchored on a center-point output
- Moves to keep value and cost honest
- Rehearsal for the stakeholder who inflates everything

## Common Traps

| AI does your ranking | Everything wins |
|---|---|
| You paste features, AI sorts | Each feature rated on its own |
| The top three become AI's pick | All of it lands high-value |
| The team never weighs in | No anchor to compare against |
| A ranking you can't defend | Cost waved away, not scoped |
|  | Nothing gets cut |

## How to Use This Prompt

1. **Before you open AI, gather your inputs.** Bring your outputs list, the product outcomes they should drive, and who will be in the room. Pick a rough candidate for the medium-value, medium-cost center point.
2. **Run the prompt to rehearse the session.** The coach helps you set up the room, anchor every output against the center point so ranking stays relative, prepare moves for when value inflates and cost gets waved off, and rehearse the hard personalities.
3. **Commit your plan, then run the room.** Lock in your facilitation plan and the center-point anchor, then lead the group to plot relative to it and pick the two or three to do first. Record the room's placements in the template ([../template.md](../template.md)).
