# The Outcome Tree Prompts

**Source:** authored for this library. Not yet in the Productside Playbook. This Play is Template 03 and 23 of 24 in the B2B work-in-progress deck (slides 11-13 and 106-108), which has no AI page for it. The prompts follow the style of the deck's own AI prompts and apply its global rule.

These are copy-paste prompts for working this Play in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use them. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a critic of the outcome tree the PM drafted, in both sections. In Section One it checks that the business outcome is measurable, that product outcomes describe what customers achieve, and that metrics are in the right place. In Section Two it asks what discovery evidence supports or contradicts each product outcome. The outcomes, targets, and the verdict on each stay the PM's.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The PM has drafted Section One, or has discovery evidence to review in Section Two. | The request is to invent the business outcome, or to fill in baselines, targets, or actuals without data. |

**Outputs to expect:** each outcome and metric checked for format and placement, the causal chain from product outcomes to business outcome tested, and in Section Two, each product outcome matched to evidence for the PM to keep, revise, or retire.

## Common Trap

**AI Writes the Tree.** The PM asks for an outcome tree for their product and gets a complete one: business outcome, five product outcomes, metrics, targets. The targets have no baseline, and the outcomes are the model's view of the business. The tree looks like strategy and is a guess.

## How to Use These Prompts

Draft your own work first, then run the prompts in order, and commit what survives to the template ([../template.md](../template.md)).

| # | Prompt | Goal | You bring | You commit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [01-check-section-one.md](01-check-section-one.md) | Check Your Outcome Tree, Section One | Your outcome and strategy discussion notes; Your draft business outcome, business success metrics, product outcomes, and product success and health metrics | A Section One outcome tree whose causal chain holds, with every metric in the right place |
| 2 | [02-review-section-two.md](02-review-section-two.md) | Review Your Outcome Tree Against Evidence, Section Two | Your Section One outcome tree; Your discovery evidence: interview findings, problem statement, solution summary, test results | Each product outcome kept, revised, or retired on evidence, with metrics updated from real data |

**Recommended tool type:** chat-based AI tool.
