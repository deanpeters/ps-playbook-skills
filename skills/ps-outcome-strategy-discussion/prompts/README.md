This is a copy-paste prompt for working this Play in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use it. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a preparation coach for the discussion, never a stand-in for the manager. It helps the PM gather what they already know, turn it into sharp, unbiased questions for each discussion area, and name the decision the discussion must reach. The answers come from the manager, in the room.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| The PM has gathered the key documents and wants to prepare questions for the discussion, or has notes from the discussion to turn into the template. | The request is to guess what the manager will say, or to fill the discussion template without the discussion happening. |

**Outputs to expect:** the PM's own questions for each discussion area, tested for bias and clarity; the business outcome stated as a measurable change; and, after the discussion, the template filled only from the PM's notes.

## Common Trap

**AI Answers for the Manager.** The PM asks the agent to fill the discussion template "with what leadership probably wants" before the conversation happens. The business outcome and success criteria are the model's guesses. The PM walks into discovery aligned with a document, not with their manager.
