# Productside Blueprint Prompts

**Source:** authored for this library. Not yet in the Productside Playbook. The Blueprint pages (1-3 of the deck) have no AI page; this prompt applies the deck's global rule.

This is a copy-paste prompt for placing your team on the Productside Blueprint in any chat-based AI tool. You don't need this skill installed to use it. If the skill *is* installed, it already behaves this way: help first, diagnosis woven in, and the next Play offered with a one-line gist.

The Playbook's rule for every AI page: **you do the thinking, and AI pressure-tests it.**

## How AI Supports This Play

AI works here as a navigator. It reads your situation, places your work on the Blueprint, names the principle at stake, and points to the next Play. Where your team really is, and what you run next, stays your call.

| Use AI when | Avoid AI when |
|---|---|
| You are unsure which phase your work is in, or what to do next. | You want AI to decide your strategy. It knows the Blueprint, not your business. |
